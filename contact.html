Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

We offer free shipping on orders over ₹999. Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days, while express delivery takes 1-2 business days.

Can I get a hair consultation?

Yes! Our hair care experts offer personalized consultations to help you choose the right products for your hair type and concerns.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for unopened products. If you're not satisfied, contact us for a full refund or exchange.

Are your products suitable for all hair types?

Our products are formulated to work with all hair types. Each product includes detailed information about which hair concerns it addresses best.
